## Operators

The measurements were carried out by the lab lead, medical physicists and students of the lab.
In the data, every operator appears only as a role code: `Lead<n>` for the lab lead, `QMP<n>`
for a Qualified Medical Physicist and `Student<n>` for students, including PhD students, student
assistants and interns (`RTT<n>` is reserved for radiation therapy technologists). The number tells people
with the same role apart and is the same in every SURF-ETDS repository. The mapping from
codes to people is kept privately by the lab and is not published.
